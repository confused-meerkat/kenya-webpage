Kenya manufacturing · the real gaps

Where the operation is leaking.

Six patterns we hear from Kenyan plant leaders before anyone mentions software. If more than two sound familiar, the rest of this page is written for you.

  1. 01

    Plan vs actual slipping

    The production plan rarely survives the shift. Attainment sits in the 60s, and nobody can tell leadership why.

  2. 02

    Low production efficiency

    OEE under 60% and nobody fully trusts the number. Capacity is paid for; too little of it reaches the dispatch bay.

  3. 03

    Changeovers eating capacity

    Too much time between SKU runs. The schedule looks fine on paper and loses an hour on the floor, every change.

  4. 04

    Material readiness

    Orders reach the line before material does. Supplier confirmations, kitting and sequencing never quite line up.

  5. 05

    Inventory & warehouse

    Stock the ERP shows that the floor can't find. Audits land the same discrepancies, quarter after quarter.

  6. 06

    Limited operational visibility

    Disconnected spreadsheets, delayed reports, no single view of what the floor is actually doing right now.
